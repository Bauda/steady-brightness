# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Assume good intent while taking reports of harm seriously.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, insults, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not spam or repeatedly derail discussions. These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect those affected.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on a comment or profile to report abuse privately to GitHub. For a concern involving this repository's moderation, you may also contact the repository owner through a contact method they publish on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. A report does not guarantee an immediate response; maintainers are volunteers.

Maintainers are expected to follow these same standards.
